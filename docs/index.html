The local validation was measuring the wrong thing

Under a hide-and-recover holdout that mirrors the organisers' masking rule, uniform random noise scores 0.1199 on the catalogue — and the field that holds 0.1563 on the leaderboard scores 0.1253. At competition emission density, coverage dominates detection. A detector that concentrates its budget on its strongest anomaly loses to one that spreads it.
